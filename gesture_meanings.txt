The webcam detector uses MediaPipe hand landmarks. Static hand poses are
recognized frame by frame; motion-based gestures such as waving, clapping,
handshakes, and writing need movement tracking for highly reliable detection.

DAILY-USE SIGN VOCABULARY (NEXT TRAINING SET)

Communication: HELLO, HI, GOOD MORNING, GOODBYE, THANK YOU, PLEASE, SORRY,
YES, NO, OKAY, HELP

Daily needs: WATER, FOOD, EAT, TEA, HUNGRY, THIRSTY, BATHROOM, SLEEP,
WAKE UP, MEDICINE, PAIN, TIRED, COLD, HOT

Emergency: HELP ME, EMERGENCY, STOP, DANGER, CALL SOMEONE, DOCTOR, POLICE,
FIRE, I AM OKAY, I NEED HELP

Sentence templates:

WATER -> I NEED WATER
BATHROOM -> I NEED TO USE THE BATHROOM
HELP -> PLEASE HELP ME
PAIN + BODY LOCATION -> I HAVE PAIN HERE

The current trained encoder contains only A-Z, del, nothing, and space. The
daily-use vocabulary above is documented for the next dataset/model training
step and is not yet recognized by the current model.
